Citizenship & Immigration FAQs

Yes, most countries allow citizenship & immigration visa holders to work part-time (usually 20 hours per week) during their studies and full-time during scheduled breaks. However, specific regulations vary by country and visa type. We'll guide you through the work authorization requirements for your chosen destination.

Financial requirements vary by country but typically include proof of funds to cover tuition fees, living expenses, and return travel costs. Generally, you need to show bank statements or sponsorship letters demonstrating sufficient funds for at least one visit. We'll help you understand the specific requirements for your chosen country.

Processing times vary by country and individual circumstances. Generally, citizenship & immigration visa applications take 4-8 weeks for most countries, but some may take longer during peak seasons. We recommend starting the application process at least 3-4 months before your intended start date to ensure sufficient time for processing.

Many countries allow citizenship & immigration visa holders to bring their spouse and dependent children on accompanying visas. However, regulations vary significantly by country and may require additional documentation and financial proof. We'll help you understand the family visa options available for your chosen destination and guide you through the application process.
