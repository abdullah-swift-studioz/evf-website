Japan Visa Overview

At E Visa Facilitation, we provide expert guidance for Japan visa applications. Japan offers various visa categories for different purposes including tourism, business, work, and study. Pakistani nationals can apply through the Japanese Embassy in Islamabad or the Consulate in Karachi. Our team has extensive experience in processing Japan visa applications and helps you prepare all required documents, ensure your application is complete, and maximize your chances of approval.

Japan Visa Process Steps

Determine Visa Type

We evaluate your profile and determine the appropriate Japan visa type for your travel purpose - tourism, business, work, or study. Each visa category has specific requirements.

Prepare Required Documents

Our experts assist in preparing all required documents including passport (valid for 6+ months), completed application form, recent photographs, travel itinerary, financial proof, and employment letter (if applicable).

Submit Application

Documents must be submitted in person at the Japanese Embassy in Islamabad or the Consulate in Karachi, or through an authorized visa agent. We ensure your application is complete and properly formatted.

Processing & Tracking

Processing time typically takes 4-5 working days for tourist visas and up to 2-3 weeks for work/business visas. We track your application status throughout the process and assist with interview preparation if required.

Receive Decision

Once your visa is approved, you'll receive your passport with the visa stamp. We guide you through collecting your documents and provide post-approval support for your travel to Japan.
