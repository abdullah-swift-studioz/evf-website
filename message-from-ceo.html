A Message from Our CEO

With 24 years of experience in the Air Express and Visa Outsourcing Business in Pakistan and UK, I have learnt that the key to success is delivering an outstanding customer experience. Clients will then keep coming back to us and recommend us to their friends and family.

This key focus is invaluable to our brand and our success. As a result, we focus on getting one thing right, employing a great team of people with dedication, integrity and an enormous set of capabilities to deliver outstanding service.

We’re a team of highly passionate professionals with a combined visa application handling experience of over six decades, who are committed to making ours a great company. That means giving you the best possible visa application services. After all, it is part of our four core values: integrity, convenience, customer-centric approach and business excellence.

We don’t always get it right, and if we ever fall short of your expectations associated with EVF, we want to learn from what went wrong. We would highly appreciate to get our applicant’s feedback and if you want to contact me directly about your own experience of our staff and the service, please email me at hashim@evisafacilitation.com
