Frequently Asked Questions

Find detailed answers to common questions about our visa facilitation services and processes.

Visa processing times vary depending on the type of visa and destination country. Generally, tourist visas take 5-15 business days, while work and study visas may take 2-8 weeks. We'll provide you with accurate timelines during your consultation based on your specific case.

Required documents typically include a valid passport, photographs, bank statements, travel itinerary, and accommodation details. Additional documents may be needed based on visa type, such as employment contracts, admission letters, or business registration. We'll provide a comprehensive checklist during your consultation.

Our service fees vary based on the type of visa, destination country, and level of assistance required. We offer transparent pricing with no hidden charges. Contact us for a detailed quote tailored to your specific requirements. We also offer package deals for families and groups.

In case of a visa refusal, we'll analyze the rejection reasons and advise on the best course of action. This may include filing an appeal, submitting a fresh application with additional documentation, or exploring alternative visa options. Our team will support you throughout the process to maximize your chances of success.
