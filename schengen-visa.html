The Schengen Area

The Schengen Area signifies a zone where 29 European countries, abolished their internal borders, for the free and unrestricted movement of people, in harmony with common rules for controlling external borders and fighting criminality by strengthening the common judicial system and police cooperation.

General Requirements

  • Valid Passport: Must be valid for at least 3 months beyond your planned departure date.
  • Visa Application Form: Fully completed and signed.
  • Photos: Two recently taken passport-sized photos.
  • Travel Insurance: Minimum coverage of €30,000 for medical emergencies.
  • Flight Itinerary: Round trip reservation or itinerary.
  • Proof of Accommodation: Hotel booking or letter of invitation.
  • Proof of Financial Means: Bank statements for the last 3-6 months.
