Germany Visa Overview

At E Visa Facilitation, we provide expert guidance for Germany visa applications. The Germany visa process involves determining your visa type, completing the application form online via VIDEX, booking an appointment through the German Foreign Office system, submitting biometrics, and tracking your application. We are an experienced visa facilitation company in Pakistan with years of proven track records, expert approach, and commitment to customers.

Germany Visa Process Steps

Determine Visa Type

Determine which visa type you need and check your eligibility. Check processing times and fees. All information for National Visas can be found on the Embassy website.

Complete Application Form

Visit VIDEX and fill the visa application form online. Print the completed form and bring it with you to the Visa Application Centre for submission.

Book Appointment

All appointments must be booked through the Appointment-System of the German Foreign Office. Book an appointment to have your fingerprints and photo taken at a Visa Application Centre for biometric information.

Submit Application & Pay Fees

Submit your complete visa application form at the Visa Application Centre in person. Pay the visa fee and VFS service fee of Euro 38 per application (PKR 12,046) in cash.

Track Your Application

You'll receive an email update when your decision is returned. Track your visa application status online using your Reference Number and last name.

Collect Your Documents

Pick up your documents from the Visa Application Centre or have them returned by courier. Bring the receipt and government identification. Representatives can collect with proper authorization.
