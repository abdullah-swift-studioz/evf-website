Meet Our Experts

Assadullah Khan

Founding Member

Assadullah Khan is a founding member of Express Visa Facilitation (EVF). He has over 30 years of leadership experience at renowned air express companies in Pakistan. He served with Gerry's/FedEx as General Manager North and played an important role in improving service standards across the organization. He was also part of the team that launched Gerry's Visa Drop Box Service (VDB) and Gerry's Courier Assisted Visa Application (CAVA) in collaboration with the US Embassy and UK High Commission in Pakistan. This unique and wide-ranging service model was then adopted by many embassies and consulates in Pakistan and around the globe. Prior to this. he worked for DHL and TCS as Area Manager. Assad moved to Canada in 2018 and started working as a freelancer with reputed immigration law firms, and gained immense hands-on experience in delivering an outstanding service and helped many families around the globe to relocate to Canada. Outside work, Assad is dedicated to his family and enjoys travelling.

Muhammad Hashim

Founding Member

Muhammad Hashim has over two decades of Air Express and BPO (business process outsourcing) experience at leading companies. He has served with Gerry's/FedEx and Gerry's Visa in the UK and Pakistan as Vice President and Head of the Visa Business Unit. He also led the Saudi visa business acquisition team and successfully completed the transition from Etimad Pvt Ltd. to Gerry's. Hashim's journey in visa services began in 2009 when he took charge as country head of UK visa operations at Gerry's. He made significant contributions in making the hub and spoke visa model of the British High Commission Islamabad successful. In 2012, he managed to set up four visa application centers for the Pakistan High Commission in London, Manchester, Birmingham, and Bradford. He is a founding member of Express Visa Facilitation (EVF) and aims to transform visa application services in the country, with a focus on maintaining the highest levels of integrity and transparency. He has a passion for helping people fulfil their travel aspirations for tourism, work, and study by making the entire process more secure and seamless. He is also committed to creating an environment at EVF that inspires collaboration and nurtures growth. Outside work, he enjoys travelling, reading and is a dedicated father and husband.
